# TrokicTech website assets

- `public/trokic.tech_logo_with_text.svg` and `public/trokic.tech_logo_no_text.svg` are the official company logos supplied by the owner. `public/trokic.tech_logo_with_text_light.svg` is the light-text version used on dark backgrounds. `public/icon.svg` is the site icon.
- `public/rallymetrica.svg` is the Rallymetrica brand mark supplied for the product feature.
- `src/images/laptop.jpg` is the photograph supplied with the licensed Tailwind Plus Studio template and used by the current company website. Unused client logos, team photographs, and other sample photographs are excluded.
- `src/fonts/Mona-Sans.var.woff2` is the self-hosted Mona Sans font. The SIL Open Font License and copyright notice are retained alongside it in `src/fonts/OFL.txt`. Upstream: https://github.com/github/mona-sans.

The customized website derives from the supplied Tailwind Plus Studio template. Its commercial terms are retained in `LICENSE.md`. This repository contains the company website as an end product; it does not contain the original template archive or a reusable template distribution.
